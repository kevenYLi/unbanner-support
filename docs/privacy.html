Privacy Policy

Unbanner for Safari · Last updated 2026-10-06

English

Unbanner does not collect, store or share any personal data.

  • The Safari extension runs only on your device. It looks for “Open in App” prompts and links to apps on the pages you visit and hides or redirects them locally. Page contents and addresses never leave your device.
  • The number of prompts removed is stored only on your device, to show that the extension is working.
  • Rule updates are downloaded from our server. These requests contain no personal information.
  • If you email us or open a GitHub issue, we use what you send only to reply and fix the problem.
  • No account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking.

Contact: unbanner.help@outlook.com

简体中文

Unbanner 不收集、不保存、不分享任何个人数据。

  • Safari 插件只在你的设备上运行：在你浏览的网页里找「打开 App」提示和跳 App 的链接，在本地隐藏或改为留在 Safari 打开。网页内容和网址不会离开你的设备。
  • 「累计去掉了多少个提示」只保存在你的设备上，用来显示插件在工作。
  • 规则更新从我们的服务器下载，请求里不含任何个人信息。
  • 你给我们发邮件或在 GitHub 提 issue 时，我们只用你提供的内容来回复和修复问题。
  • 没有账号、没有统计分析、没有广告、没有追踪。

联系：unbanner.help@outlook.com
